## METHODOLOGY

Kubelka-Munk theory describes paint opacity using two coefficients:
- **K** (Absorption coefficient): How much light is absorbed
- **S** (Scattering coefficient): How much light is scattered

The K/S ratio determines the color, while S determines hiding power.

### Relationships:
- High S = Good hiding power = Opaque
- Low S = Poor hiding power = Transparent
- High K = Strong color absorption
- K≈0 for whites (no color absorption)

### Typical Ranges by Pigment Type:

**White Pigments:**
- K ≈ 0 (no color absorption)
- S = 1.5-3.0 (excellent scattering)
- Titanium dioxide (Rutile): S = 2.5-3.0 (highest)
- Zinc oxide: S = 1.5-2.0 (moderate)

**Black Pigments:**
- K = 2.0-3.0 (maximum absorption)
- S = 0.8-1.5 (moderate scattering)
- Carbon blacks: Higher K, lower S
- Iron oxide blacks: Moderate K and S

**Colored Pigments (General):**
- K = 0.3-2.0 (varies by color intensity)
- S = 0.1-1.5 (varies by opacity)
- Transparent pigments: S < 0.3
- Semi-opaque: S = 0.3-0.8
- Opaque: S > 0.8

**Iron Oxides (Common in our palette):**
- Generally semi-opaque to opaque
- K = 0.5-1.2 (moderate absorption)
- S = 0.2-0.5 (moderate scattering)
- Red: Higher K, lower S (more transparent)
- Yellow: Lower K, moderate S
- Brown: Moderate K and S
